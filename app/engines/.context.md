Pure-Python scoring engines with no Streamlit dependency. Consumed by `modules/score_propensao.py` and `modules/plano_comercial.py`. Tested directly by `tests/test_propensao_engine.py`.

## Summary

- `propensao_engine.py` — `PropensaoEngine`: 5-component vectorized purchase propensity score (Recency 30pt, Frequency 25pt, Seasonality 15pt, Volume 20pt, Trend 10pt); applies recency penalty multipliers; outputs tier (QUENTE/MORNO/FRIO/DORMENTE)
- `sazonalidade_semanal.py` — `SemanalPropensaoEngine`: adjusts base propensity score by within-month purchase seasonality (W=0.40 weight); wraps `PropensaoEngine` output

## Constraints

- MUST NOT import `streamlit` — engines must remain testable without a Streamlit session
- Score is absolute (not min-max normalized) by design — do not add normalization
