<context path="app/utils" updated="2026-05-30">

Shared helpers imported by `dashboard.py` and all modules. No business logic — only infrastructure (data loading, auth, visual tokens, calendar math).

## Summary

- `data_loader.py` — `load_vendas()` and `load_meta()`: auto-detects local parquet vs. Google Drive (via `st.secrets[gdrive]`); cached with `@st.cache_data(ttl=3600)`
- `auth.py` — bcrypt-based login gate: `require_auth()`, `render_sidebar_user()`, `logout()`; brute-force lockout after 5 failed attempts
- `visual.py` — color palette constants (COR_PRIMARIA etc.), `fmt_ton()`, `cor_ritmo()`, `icone_ritmo()`, `cor_bg_ritmo()`, `seta_tendencia()`
- `calendar_tw.py` — ISO 8601 Technical Week calendar: `get_month_tw_ranges()`, `day_to_semana_tw()`, `vectorize_semana_tw()` (vectorized, ~50× faster than apply), `tw_label()`

## Constraints

- MUST NOT contain business logic (rules belong in `config.py` or engines)
- `calendar_tw.py` MUST NOT be modified without updating all callers; it is a shared contract
